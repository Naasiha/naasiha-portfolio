Multi-location store launch strategy.

Chicking UK / Grand openings & anniversaries

£6,500revenue on Wellingborough's opening day, with queues outside

01 — Business objective

Build awareness in towns where Chicking was opening for the first time, maximise opening-day footfall, and give established stores a reason to celebrate.

02 — My role

Campaign strategy, creative direction, coordination with franchise owners, media and creators, and on-the-ground execution.

03 — Strategy

Hyperlocal launch plans adapted to each town: press and radio outreach, food creator partnerships, students' union and community promotions, launch offers such as the £1 burger deal, and geo-targeted TikTok ads.

04 — Execution

Five store openings (Wellingborough, Newport, Clarkston, Sighthill and Hamilton) and one anniversary event (Wolverhampton's 1st). Invitation flyers, posters, press releases, radio, creator visits and in-store marketing.

05 — Results

Wellingborough took £6,500 on opening day (above). For Newport, I pitched 10 local news outlets and secured coverage in the South Wales Argus and Newport Chronicle. Launches were also promoted on Beat Route Radio (Wellingborough) and WCR FM (Wolverhampton).

06 — Key learnings

I turned what worked into a grand opening playbook, so future franchise launches can repeat the approach rather than start from scratch.
